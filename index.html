I am an M.Sc. candidate in Urban Planning at Peking University (Smart City & Big Data Program), and currently work as a Research Assistant in the Data Science Department at Duke Kunshan University. I completed my B.Sc. in Traffic Engineering at Beijing Jiaotong University (Minor in Finance), graduating as a Beijing Outstanding Graduate.

My core vision is bridging the gap between spatiotemporal city systems and advanced artificial intelligence (Geo-AI). I develop innovative frameworks integrating **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, **Spatio-Temporal Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)**, and **Causal Inference** to model reliable, sustainable, and cognitive-map aware urban neighborhoods and travel dynamics.
