I am an incoming Ph.D. student (starting June 2026) in the Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at the University of Southern California (USC), where I am honored to be advised by Prof. Ruolin Li. I completed my M.Sc. in Urban Planning (Smart City & Big Data Program) at Peking University, and currently serve as a Research Assistant in the Data Science Department at Duke Kunshan University. I received my B.Sc. in Traffic Engineering from Beijing Jiaotong University (Minor in Finance), graduating as a Beijing Outstanding Graduate.

My research lies at the intersection of Urban Spatiotemporal Mobility Intelligence and Decision Policy AI. I formulate advanced mathematical and machine learning architectures—including **Large Language Models (LLMs)**, **Causal Inference**, **Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)**, and **Mathematical Programming**—to synthesize realistic travel behaviors, establish metropolitan digital twins, and drive trustworthy infrastructural decisions.

Core Research Directions

AI-Empowered Mobility Intelligence

Building transferrable foundation and generative models (integrating GPS, graph neural networks, and semantic social media signals) to synthesize and classify realistic spatial-temporal human travel behaviors.

Urban Spatiotemporal Digital Twins

Developing city-scale interactive simulation systems that combine Multi-Agent reinforcement learning coordination and cognitive-map aware community platforms (such as AskNearby) to evaluate traffic bottlenecks.

Causal Policy & Trustworthy AI

Translating spatial AI outputs into decision-ready tools using Difference-in-Differences and Interrupted Time Series models to diagnose EV adoption car rebound and evaluate checkpoints satisfaction.
